\section{Teamwork and Individual Contributions}
\label{sec:teamwork}

\guide{
The brief's individual-contribution requirement covers eight
deliverable areas (Abstract / Lit Review, System Design, Hardware,
Software, Mechanical/CAD, Testing, Teamwork, Report Writing). Map
each member to the areas they led. The 1st+ rubric wants
``exceptional teamwork with outstanding coordination and equal
contribution.''

For top marks, include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item A contribution table (member $\rightarrow$ areas led).
  \item One paragraph per member describing primary contributions,
        impact on the team, and any cross-area support given.
  \item A short paragraph on team process: how tasks were allocated,
        how blockers were escalated, how decisions were recorded.
  \item Brief mention that confidential peer feedback was submitted
        independently to the module lead via Canvas, as required.
\end{itemize}
Avoid: blame, vague contributions like ``helped with everything'',
or unbalanced framing.
